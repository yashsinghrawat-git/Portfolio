🛡️ Risk Analysis

Real-Time Risk Dashboard
for NBFC Loan Portfolio

Designed and deployed a dynamic risk monitoring dashboard for a ₹200 Cr NBFC loan book. The system tracks NPA levels, concentration risk, credit scores, early warning indicators, and Value-at-Risk (VaR) in real time using Python and Power BI.

📅 Duration: 10 Weeks (2023)
👤 Role: Risk Analyst & Data Architect
🏢 Client: NBFC (Retail Lending)
🛠️ Tools: Python, Power BI, SQL, Excel

🎯 Project Overview

An NBFC with ₹200 Cr in disbursed retail loans (personal loans, SME loans, and vehicle finance) was struggling with manual, Excel-based risk tracking that was outdated and error-prone. They needed a real-time, automated risk monitoring solution.

I designed and built a full-stack risk intelligence system: Python scripts for data processing and risk calculations, connected to a Power BI dashboard that refreshes every 4 hours with live loan data from their core banking system (CBS).

💡 Key Result: NPA detection time reduced from 7 days (manual) to 4 hours (automated). 15 early-warning loan accounts flagged in the first month, avoiding an estimated ₹3.2 Cr in potential NPAs.

🔧 System Architecture

  1. Data Ingestion (Python + SQL): Automated ETL pipeline pulls fresh loan data from the CBS every 4 hours. Handles data cleaning, deduplication, and schema normalization for 12,000+ active loan accounts.
  2. Risk Scoring Engine (Python): Calculates 8 risk metrics per account — DPD bucket, FOIR, LTV ratio, EMI bounce frequency, bureau score change, sector exposure, and collateral coverage ratio.
  3. VaR Calculation: Historical simulation and parametric VaR computed daily for the overall portfolio at 95% and 99% confidence levels. Stress testing against 2008 GFC and COVID-19 scenarios.
  4. Early Warning System (EWS): Rule-based flags + ML anomaly detection (Isolation Forest) to identify deteriorating accounts 30–60 days before formal DPD classification.
  5. Power BI Dashboard: Interactive dashboards with drill-down by product, geography, vintage, and risk band. Executive summary, analyst drill-down, and collection team views.

🔑 Key Risk Findings

  • Personal Loan Segment: Highest NPA at 4.2%. Root cause: high FOIR (>55%) borrowers and bureau score below 680 at origination. Recommended stricter underwriting criteria.
  • Geographic Concentration: 38% of the book concentrated in 2 districts — flagged as concentration risk; recommended cap of 25% per district.
  • Vintage Analysis: Loans disbursed in Q3 2021 showing 2x higher 90-DPD rates — correlated with a collection gap during COVID recovery period.
  • EWS Accuracy: Early warning model identified 80% of eventual NPA accounts 45 days in advance, giving the collection team sufficient lead time.

✅ Outcomes

The dashboard is now a core operational tool for the NBFC's credit and collection teams. Key outcomes:

  • NPA ratio reduced from 4.8% to 3.1% over 12 months post-deployment
  • Early warning system saved approximately ₹3.2 Cr in potential write-offs
  • Regulatory reporting (RBI IRACP) time cut from 3 days to 4 hours
  • Collection efficiency improved by 22% due to timely flagging of at-risk accounts

📌 NBFC's board approved expansion of the dashboard framework to cover their newly launched MSME lending vertical in 2024.
