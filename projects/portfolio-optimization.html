₹50 Cr Multi-Asset Strategy

Designed and implemented a scientifically diversified multi-asset portfolio for a High Net-Worth Individual client using the Markowitz Mean-Variance framework, achieving 22.4% annual return while maintaining a Sharpe Ratio of 1.84.

🎯 Project Overview

A high-net-worth business owner approached me with a ₹50 Cr corpus that was predominantly sitting in FDs and low-yield debt. The goal was to restructure the portfolio to generate inflation-beating, tax-efficient returns while preserving capital and managing downside risk.

Over the 2-year period since restructuring, the portfolio has delivered 22.4% CAGR, significantly outperforming the client's previous FD-heavy approach and the benchmark Nifty 500 TRI (16.8% in the same period).

💡 Key Result: Portfolio grew from ₹50 Cr to ₹74.5 Cr in 2 years, generating ₹24.5 Cr in absolute returns. Sharpe Ratio: 1.84 — well above the target of 1.5.

🔬 Key Strategic Decisions

  • Tactical Overweight in IT & Pharma (Q4 2022): Identified sector-level undervaluation and increased equity allocation from 40% to 55% temporarily. Reversed post re-rating.
  • REIT Addition: Added Embassy Office Parks REIT for 7.5% yield with capital appreciation potential — key diversifier against equity volatility.
  • Sovereign Gold Bond SGB: Tax-efficient gold exposure with 2.5% sovereign interest on top of gold price appreciation.
  • Systematic Rebalancing: Quarterly rebalancing back to target weights, with a 5% drift trigger for mid-period rebalances.
  • Tax Harvesting: Executed strategic loss harvesting in February 2023 to offset LTCG from equity gains, saving ₹18 lakh in taxes.

  • Client's income from portfolio (dividends + coupons + REIT distributions): ~₹2.4 Cr/year
