Indian IT Sector Deep Dive:
Buy/Sell Signal Generation

A comprehensive fundamental and technical analysis of 5 leading Indian IT companies — TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL Tech, and Tech Mahindra — with 12-month price targets and actionable buy/sell signals.

👤 Role: Lead Analyst

🛠️ Tools: Bloomberg, Python, Excel, DCF

🎯 Project Overview

This project was commissioned by an institutional HNI fund seeking a structured, data-backed entry framework into India's IT sector following the post-pandemic correction. The analysis covered 5 large-cap IT stocks representing ₹2,000 Cr+ market cap companies.

The goal was to combine fundamental valuation with technical trend analysis to generate clear, actionable BUY / HOLD / SELL recommendations with defined price targets and stop-loss levels.

💡 Key Result: 4 out of 5 price targets were achieved within the 12-month window, generating an average portfolio alpha of +8.6% over the Nifty IT index.

🔬 Methodology

The research followed a three-tier framework combining quantitative and qualitative analysis:

  1. Macro & Sector Screening – USD/INR sensitivity, global IT spending forecasts (Gartner), BPO vs product mix analysis, and management commentary review.
  2. Fundamental Valuation – DCF models with 3 revenue growth scenarios (Bear/Base/Bull), P/E band analysis, EV/EBITDA, ROE, ROCE, and dividend yield screening.
  3. Technical Confirmation – 200-day EMA position, RSI, MACD cross, volume divergence, Fibonacci retracement levels, and support/resistance mapping.

Stocks were rated only when both fundamental undervaluation AND technical trend confirmation were simultaneously present — reducing false positives significantly.

🔑 Key Findings

  • Indian IT sector was trading at a 15–22% discount to its 5-year average P/E multiple at the time of analysis, indicating sector-wide undervaluation.
  • HCL Tech showed the strongest FCF yield (5.8%) among peers, combined with a strong orderbook backlog in infrastructure services.
  • TCS's enterprise deal pipeline and strong deal conversion ratio supported a premium valuation despite global headwinds.
  • Wipro's segment margin decline (-180bps YoY) and elevated attrition made it a SELL — confirmed by bearish MACD divergence on weekly charts.
  • Tech Mahindra's HOLD rating was conservative given ongoing BPO restructuring and CEO transition uncertainty.

✅ Conclusion & Impact

The equity research framework delivered strong results, with 4 out of 5 recommendations hitting their targets within the 12-month window. The institutional fund achieved +18.4% annualised return on the IT bucket of its portfolio, outperforming the Nifty IT index by +8.6 percentage points.

📌 This project reinforced the importance of requiring dual confirmation (fundamental + technical) before initiating positions. The one miss (Tech Mahindra) had mixed fundamental signals that in hindsight should have been rated SELL, not HOLD.
