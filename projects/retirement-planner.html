Retirement & Wealth Preservation
Plan for Senior Professional

Crafted a comprehensive 20-year retirement and wealth preservation roadmap for a retiring senior IAS officer. The plan integrates SWP strategy, tax-efficient withdrawal planning, inflation-adjusted corpus projections, and a Monte Carlo simulation for retirement security analysis.

📅 Duration: 4 Weeks (2023)
👤 Role: Financial Planner & Wealth Advisor
🏢 Client: Senior IAS Officer (Retiring)
🛠️ Tools: Excel, Python, Monte Carlo

🎯 Project Overview

A senior IAS officer approaching retirement at age 58 came to me with ₹2.8 Cr in accumulated savings, a government pension, and significant anxiety about outliving their money. The family consisted of the client, spouse (56), and one dependent child.

The brief was clear: ensure complete financial security for 30+ years, maintain lifestyle standards (monthly expense: ₹1.2 lakh), fund one international trip per year, cover child's higher education (₹45 lakh in 3 years), and leave a meaningful legacy.

💡 Key Result: Monte Carlo simulation showed 94.6% probability of corpus surviving 30 years. Client's estate projected at ₹4.8 Cr at age 88 under base case assumptions.

📐 The Retirement Corpus Architecture

The ₹2.8 Cr corpus was divided into three distinct buckets — each serving a different time horizon and purpose:

Bucket Purpose Allocation Investment Vehicle Target Return
🪣 Bucket 1 (0–3 Yrs) Immediate expenses ₹35 Lakh Liquid Fund + FD Ladder 6.5–7%
🪣 Bucket 2 (4–12 Yrs) Child education + medium-term ₹95 Lakh Balanced Adv. MF + NCDs 10–12%
🪣 Bucket 3 (12+ Yrs) Legacy + inflation protection ₹1.5 Cr Equity Funds + SGBs + REIT 14–18%

Additional income source: Government pension of ₹65,000/month (indexed to DA) supplements the SWP withdrawals significantly.

📈 Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) Design

Designed a SWP from Bucket 2 mutual funds to supplement pension and cover monthly expenses:

  • Monthly SWP: ₹45,000/month from balanced advantage funds — tax-efficient LTCG structure after 1 year.
  • Step-up SWP: 5% annual increase in withdrawal to keep pace with lifestyle inflation.
  • Tax Efficiency: SWP withdrawals structured to keep annual LTCG below ₹1 lakh exemption limit in the first 5 years.
  • Pension + SWP = ₹1.1 lakh/month in year 1, growing to ₹1.6 lakh by year 10.

🏛️ Estate & Legacy Planning

  • Will & Nomination: Assisted client in documenting a comprehensive will and ensuring all financial assets had updated nominations — covering bank accounts, MF folios, insurance policies, and property.
  • Life Insurance Review: Existing term policy reviewed and found adequate. Recommended ₹25 lakh critical illness rider addition.
  • Health Insurance: Super Top-Up policy of ₹50 lakh recommended to cover rising medical expenses in later years without eroding corpus.
  • Sovereign Gold Bonds: ₹20 lakh in SGBs held for tax-free maturity gains (8-year lock-in) — part of legacy bucket.
  • Joint Account & POA: Recommended joint account setup and spouse financial empowerment — ensuring spouse can manage finances independently.

💡 Tax Optimization Strategy

  • Section 80C fully utilized: PPF + ELSS + life insurance premium = ₹1.5 lakh deduction.
  • Section 80D: Health insurance premiums for self + spouse = ₹50,000 deduction.
  • SWP timing to maximize LTCG exemption — staggered across 2 financial years where needed.
  • Interest income from FDs restructured across HUF and spouse name to reduce slab rate impact.
  • SGB redemption at maturity — completely tax-free on capital gains as per Income Tax Act.
