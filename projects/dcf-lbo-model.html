3-Statement & LBO Model
for Mid-Cap Manufacturing Acquisition

Developed an integrated 3-statement financial model, DCF valuation, and Leveraged Buyout (LBO) model to evaluate the acquisition feasibility of a mid-cap Indian manufacturing company. Identified a 35% undervaluation that led to a successful acquisition recommendation.

📅 Duration: 8 Weeks (2022)
👤 Role: Lead Financial Modeller
🏢 Client: Private Equity Fund
🛠️ Tools: Excel, Python, Bloomberg

🎯 Project Overview

A mid-sized private equity fund engaged me to evaluate the acquisition of a listed Indian manufacturing company in the auto-ancillary space. The target company had ₹850 Cr in revenue and strong free cash flow generation despite a depressed market valuation.

I built a comprehensive financial model from scratch — covering the 3-statement model (IS, BS, CF), standalone DCF, peer comparison, and a full LBO model — to determine if the acquisition would generate sufficient equity returns for the PE fund's 5-year exit strategy.

💡 Key Result: Model identified the company trading at a 35% discount to intrinsic value. PE fund proceeded with acquisition. Projected 5-year IRR: 28.4%. MOIC: 3.2x.

🏗️ Model Architecture

The model was structured in 6 integrated sheets:

  1. Assumptions Dashboard: All inputs in one place — revenue growth rates, margin assumptions, capex, D&A, working capital days, debt terms, and exit multiples.
  2. 3-Statement Model: Fully integrated P&L, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement with automatic BS balancing and CFS reconciliation.
  3. DCF Valuation: 5-year FCFF projection with 3 scenarios (Bear/Base/Bull), WACC calculation, and Terminal Value using both Gordon Growth and Exit Multiple methods.
  4. Comparable Company Analysis (CCA): EV/EBITDA, P/E, P/Sales, EV/Sales comparison vs 8 listed peers. Football field summary.
  5. LBO Model: Entry assumptions, debt waterfall, PIK interest, management rollover, equity sponsor returns, and 3/5/7-year exit scenarios.
  6. Returns Summary: IRR sensitivity tables (entry EV/EBITDA vs exit EV/EBITDA), MOIC bridge, and LP/GP distribution waterfall.

✅ Outcome

The PE fund proceeded with the acquisition based on the model's findings. The company was acquired at ₹1,660 Cr EV (6.2x EBITDA). Post-acquisition, three value creation levers identified in the model — margin improvement, working capital optimization, and a new EV supply contract — have all been initiated.

📌 The model is now used as the standard acquisition evaluation template for the fund's future manufacturing sector deals. The fund has since completed 2 additional acquisitions using this framework.
